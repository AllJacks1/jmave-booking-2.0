## 1. Quick quotation (estimate)

One page. Title **"Rental Estimate"**. No client details required (optional name).

- Service (Self-Drive / With Driver), vehicle or body type + category, dates and times, days/hours, destination.
- Line items with remarks, subtotal, discounts, total.
- For Self-Drive: deposit amount as an information line ("Refundable security deposit upon release: ₱2,000").
- Note: "This is an estimate. Rates are confirmed once a formal quotation is issued."

## 2. Formal quotation — Self-Drive

Title **"Self-Driven Rental Quotation"**. Mirrors the old `CLIENTS COPY` sheet:

1. **Booking status** badge: `Pending` → text "Your reservation has not yet been confirmed. Please settle the required reservation payment to secure your booking." / `Confirmed` → "Your transaction was successful. See you soon!"
2. **Rental details:** booking date, vehicle release date + time, return date + time, CDW (With / Without).
3. **Vehicle details:** car (body type or specific vehicle), category, time duration, days.
4. **Client details:** name, Facebook name.
5. **Authorized area of use:** from, to.
6. **Detailed breakdown of charges:** every engine line with its remark (e.g. `(2,630 × 1) = 2,630`), then discounts, then **Rental total**.
7. **Deposit:** refundable security deposit, with: "Refunded after vehicle inspection, provided there are no unpaid charges, damages, traffic violations, or other deductions under the Rental Agreement."
8. **Amounts payable:** "After payment, please send your proof of payment to your Booking Consultant for verification." Reservation fee (today), Delivery fee (today), Balance on release (net of RF + DF), Total contract value. Terminal fees if card.
9. **Booking information** (bullets):
   - This quotation is based on the selected rental period, vehicle category, and destination provided by the client.
   - The quoted rates apply only to the rental details stated in this quotation.
   - Full payment and the required refundable security deposit must be settled before vehicle release unless otherwise agreed upon in writing.
   - Any changes to the rental period, pickup or return schedule, destination, or vehicle category may result in additional charges or a revised quotation.
   - Additional charges may apply for excess mileage, rental extensions, late vehicle return, fuel shortages, damages, traffic violations, parking fees, toll fees, and other applicable charges in accordance with the Rental Agreement.
   - Self-Drive rentals include a 150 km daily mileage allowance. Excess mileage shall be charged at the prevailing J-MaVe Cars rate.
   - This quotation is valid for 24 hours from the date of issuance unless otherwise stated.
   - Vehicle reservation shall be considered confirmed only upon receipt and verification of the required booking payment.
   - Vehicle availability is not guaranteed until the booking has been confirmed.
   - Vehicle assignment may change due to operational requirements.
   - If the reserved vehicle becomes unavailable due to unforeseen circumstances, J-MaVe Cars reserves the right to provide a similar or higher-category vehicle at no additional cost, subject to availability.
10. **Valid until** date + time. **Your booking consultant:** name.
11. Closing: "Thank you for choosing J-MaVe Cars. We appreciate your trust and look forward to providing you with a safe, convenient, and reliable car rental experience."

## 3. Formal quotation — With Driver

Title **"Quotation — With Driver"**. Mirrors `CLIENTS COPY WDRIVER`:

1. **Reservation status** badge. Pending text: "Vehicle is NOT reserved until booking has been confirmed and booking requirements have been completed."
2. Booking date, travel date.
3. **Travel details:** quotation number, car, pricing basis (All-in / Renter's fuel), reserved package in consecutive hours, service area, number of days, trip type.
4. **Trip details:** pick-up place, drop-off place, pick-up date, drop-off date, pick-up time, scheduled return time, itinerary.
5. **Client details:** name. **Driver details:** name + contact, or "To be assigned upon confirmation".
6. **Charges:** show the full line breakdown (new: the old client copy only showed the total). Include Rounding adjustment and Service fee lines. Then Sub total, 3.5% terminal fee (if card), corporate mark-up (if any), **Grand total**.
7. **Supplemental charges** (not in total): Excess hour `<body type> ₱<rate> / hr` (renter's fuel or all-in rate), Night differential "10:01 PM – 5:59 AM, 10% / hour", Parking and toll fees "Actual cost", Incidental charges "Actual cost".
8. **Booking information** (bullets):
   - This quotation is based on the itinerary and schedule provided by the client.
   - The quoted rate is based on the selected rental package and itinerary provided by the client.
   - Full payment is required before the commencement of the trip unless otherwise agreed.
   - Changes to the itinerary, destinations, pickup/return schedule, rental duration, or standby requirements may require a revised quotation.
   - Overtime, additional stops, excess mileage, and other services shall be charged accordingly.
   - This quotation is valid for 24 hours unless otherwise stated.
   - Vehicle reservation becomes effective only after the required booking requirements have been completed and payment has been confirmed, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
9. **Package descriptions** (print the one that applies):
   - **Round Trip:** Driver stays with you for the entire active contract (hours or full 24 hrs). Multiple destinations allowed (Point A → B → C). Driver does not return to the office while the contract is active. If charged per hours or 24 hrs, the driver may return to the office only after the contract ends. If there's a new contract the next day, the driver will come back to your location.
   - **Pick up / Drop off:** The service covers one direct route only, from Point A → Point B. Side trips, additional stops, waiting time, or changes in route are not included in this contract type. Any request beyond the agreed destination will be subject to additional charges.
   - **Airport Transfer:** Route is strictly from Airport → Point B (e.g., hotel or residence). Side trips, additional stops, waiting time, or changes in route are not included in this contract type. Any request beyond the agreed destination will be subject to additional charges.
10. Valid until, booking consultant, closing, footer as above.
